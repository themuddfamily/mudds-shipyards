## Exact detention actions, authorization and recovery

There is one cell and no manifest/list of prisoners. The candidate compartment owner keeps only the active vessel/entity generation, current detainee avatar/peer and chair-claim generation, consent, actual door state and current transition sequence. These are transient runtime state, not a new persistence schema, criminal record or global authority framework.

1. **Open/volunteer.** Initial cell door is open. Docked at its own or another accepted berth, landed and engine OFFLINE, a player walks through the open doorway. At the empty detainee chair the ordinary remappable **Interact** prompt reads `VOLUNTEER FOR SECURE TRANSPORT — INTERACT TO SIT; INTERACT TO WITHDRAW; FIRE TO RELEASE`. Interact claims that exact chair through current authority and explicitly consents to this trip; no external actor may enrol another player. A normal generic passenger-seat prompt is insufficient consent. A second claimant, already-seated actor, wrong body/ship, stale generation or occupied chair refuses without mutation. Consent creates `CONSENT READY`, not a sealed cell.
2. **Guard claim/secure.** Another player physically claims `guard_station` with Interact; chair label is `GUARD`. From this chair only, a rising edge of existing remappable **FIRE** requests `SECURE CELL` when `CONSENT READY`. Host checks current embodied guard claim, current consenting detainee in its exact chair, finite/current entity/claim/sequence, both bodies inside their correct cabin regions, landed/OFFLINE, cell opening/sweep clear and no destruction/reset/transition pending. It then closes the internal door; only complete CLOSED with matching collision commits `SECURED`. No pilot/ordinary passenger/client boolean can grant this permission. A refusal leaves the last safe state unchanged; withdrawal during closing wins over sealing.
3. **Transport.** Pilot walks to and claims helm normally; start/flight/landing controls remain existing pilot authority. Empty-cell flight is allowed. With an enrolled detainee, launch requires guard occupied and actual `SECURED`; the pilot cannot force seal or consent. External hatch follows ordinary flight safety. Secure door actually prevents ordinary walking passage; it is not a HUD flag. Detainee movement/seat/aim cannot drive, fire a weapon, repair, open external hatch, confiscate items or earn rewards. No actor may be simultaneously pilot/guard/detainee. Leaving helm gives no implicit control to another occupant.
4. **Guard release.** While `SECURED` or closing, a guard FIRE rising edge requests `RELEASE CELL`, including in flight. Authority cancels securing and opens the internal door normally, reporting RELEASING then RELEASED/OPEN; physical passage becomes clear only at actual OPEN. Guard standing (Interact), disconnecting, being destroyed/removed, or losing its claim instead triggers the fail-open release below. Inflight internal release does not require an external hatch opening or stop the moving cabin.
5. **Detainee release/stand.** While seated, detainee FIRE means **immediate emergency release**, not weapon fire. It revokes consent, cancels all pending seal requests and sets the internal door fully OPEN with matching clear collision in the authoritative update. The avatar may stay sitting, with no active detention enrolment. Interact while enrolled means withdraw consent **and** stand: same immediate fail-open first, then normal seat release/physical stand to the supported inside-cell pose. It never teleports across the wall. If the stand pose is obstructed, remain safely seated with door open and stand retry available; consent is already gone and guard cannot reseal. A fresh consent needs a new ordinary leave/reclaim of the chair at docked/OFFLINE state. Holding FIRE cannot alternate seal/release; only sequenced rising-edge requests apply. No remapped input may bypass this exit option.
6. **Disembark/reuse.** At strict dock, neutral automatic shutdown must reach OFFLINE. Internal release, physical stand, walk through open cell door to aisle and stern, open external hatch under safe conditions, and walk onto station. Consent is trip-local and never survives disembark. Guard/detainee can exchange roles only through release, physical walk and fresh authorized chair claim; a detention actor cannot steal helm from its current occupant. No role reward is generated.

The guard FIRE action is intentionally a **new bounded candidate-specific consumer** of existing remappable input. It is not an existing `CrewRoleGameplayProfile` action: that profile only knows pilot/gunner/passenger/engineer intents, and passenger capability does not authorize cell operation. Candidate-scoped GameFlow routing and a narrow network request/response path must explicitly connect these inputs to the compartment owner. Keep existing E-to-stand behavior available; HUD must show actual configured FIRE/Interact bindings for guard and detainee. Unrelated ships retain their normal fire/seat actions. Guard/detainee `passenger_ping`/cargo-manifest receipts get no execution consumer here.

**Fail-open and interruption:** loss of either enrolled body's current claim/connection, reset/destruction, consent withdrawal, migration/host loss or unusable compartment state retires enrolment and pending transitions before another command can seal. In a surviving cabin, snap internal door fully open with collider/panel agreement and preserve supported movement; release stale seats/relationships through their owners. If no trustworthy supported ship/session remains, recover each player through existing supported station/on-foot recovery composition. Never open the external hatch in flight or drop a body into space as release. On host loss, a peer must not wait forever for an absent authority to acknowledge emergency release: retire the unusable session under existing session-recovery policy, with no local takeover of ship/detention authority. An actual timeout/recovery binding is required, not assumed from a UI prompt. Late old-host packets and old seal requests cannot re-enrol/relock a recovered or reused body. No saved detention state, forced arrest or persistent prisoner ownership exists.

## Damage, service, destruction, save and reuse

Use existing HeroShip/combat component owners for hull, engine/steering and applicable fitted component consequences; no weapon consequence is claimed for an unarmed craft. Actual engine/steering damage must change play and normal station service must restore it. Sitting/volunteering/releasing does not heal or reset components. The cell lock has no new damage/repair minigame: destruction/unusable authority fails it open; ordinary component damage must never disable the occupant's release path.

Destruction stops propulsion/control and cell actions, retires pilot/guard/detainee claims and moving relationships, and uses supported occupant recovery rather than leaving players in a deleted ship. Recall/regeneration uses HeroShip `preflight_reset_for_reuse()` / `commit_reset_for_reuse()` and actual home-berth lease. Stale, ineligible or occupied-berth refusal is atomic: no partial hull heal, changed consent, released body into space or duplicate craft. Safety-triggered release remains available independently of a refused reset. Successful reset yields exactly one usable candidate at its own berth, normal health/component policy, new occupancy generation, cell fully open/unenrolled, hatch/ramp matching actual state, and no stale controls or old-host claims. Candidate preflight/commit hooks must include the compartment's real state.

**Proposed restart policy:** use existing GameFlow `SOLO_SAFE_RECOVERY_SLOT` and UserDataStore safe on-foot continuation. A fresh process resumes the local player at the existing supported station recovery context with unrelated inventory/settings/progress conserved; candidate becomes available through normal Main/home-berth lifecycle. No in-flight seat, cell lock, consent, authority or durable criminal state resumes. This is safe recovery, not an in-flight position/damage continuation promise. Refused/interrupted save keeps the last committed record/retry path; it cannot lose progress, mint resources or strand/duplicate a body. Prove fresh-process committed recovery and refused/interrupted boundaries. Owner interpretation approval must cover these semantics; broader save/recovery obligations remain open.
