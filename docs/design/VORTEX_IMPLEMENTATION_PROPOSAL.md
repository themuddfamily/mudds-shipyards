## One fixed modern trial design

**Every number, shape, fitting and action below is a new design proposal. None is an observation of historical Vortex or an accepted multiplayer capacity.** These are the baseline to approve or reject together. Change a failed design through a named revision/decision; do not quietly relax acceptance to fit it.

**Intended use:** a short-hop people carrier with one continuous stern ramp → aisle → helm route and four ordinary passenger chairs. Players embark, travel and disembark physically using existing transport primitives. “Troop” names the supported broad role; it adds no troop NPCs, squad AI, recruitment, deployment orders, combat bonuses, missions, rewards, drop pods or new transport simulation. Its weakness is modest speed/agility and a large landing footprint, with no cargo utility or specialist stations. It should feel more responsive locally than Halyard while losing Halyard's 108 cruise speed and gunner/engineer/Loadmaster/bunk capabilities; Jovian retains freight and engineer use. Four chairs do not make Vortex a supported five-player session.

## Damage, repair, recovery, save and reuse contract

Use HeroShip and current combat component owners for hull/component damage, engine/steering/weapon consequences and normal service repair. No passenger engineer repair or free healing on sit/stand. Pilot fire must actually damage a production damageable target once through the combat owner. Show at least engine and weapon consequences and service restoration before full acceptance; apply every component consequence fitted by the actual candidate. Clearing presentation alone is not repair.

Destruction retires every pilot/passenger claim and moving-interior relationship, stops controls/fire, and returns occupants to the existing safe supported recovery context. Ordinary recall/regeneration uses HeroShip reset preflight/commit and the exact home-berth lease; a stale/reset-ineligible/occupied-berth request must refuse without partial mutation. Success yields exactly one usable candidate at its own berth, with the existing normal reset policy for health/components, and fresh occupancy generation. Old seat/action/relationship packets cannot reacquire it. Hatch/ramp/seat visuals and moving-frame tracking reset with their real state.

**Proposed restart policy:** use the existing GameFlow `SOLO_SAFE_RECOVERY_SLOT`/UserDataStore safe on-foot continuation, not new durable in-flight passenger simulation. After restart or unusable session recovery, the local player starts at the existing supported station recovery location, with no saved seat/helm authority; Vortex is available through the normal clean Main/home-berth lifecycle. This is safe recovery, not a promise to resume an in-flight location, damage state or passenger arrangement. Existing unrelated settings/inventory/progress must be conserved. Failed/refused save retains the last committed recovery record and its retry path; it cannot strand the player, mint resources, restore a stale claim or create duplicate ships. Check both a fresh process after a committed record and a refused-write/interrupted boundary. The policy must be included in the owner's interpretation approval; it does not waive broader project save/recovery gates.
