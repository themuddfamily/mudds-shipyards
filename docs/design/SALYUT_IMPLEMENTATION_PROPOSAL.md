## One fixed modern trial design

**Intended use:** a compact, unarmed local people shuttle with one pilot and **two actual physical passenger chairs**. Its transport function is demonstrated by real embodied people walking aboard, claiming separate chairs, travelling, standing/reseating, handing over the helm and walking out after docking. A chair count is a proposed fitout, not approved session capacity. There are no NPC passengers, transport missions, fares, rewards, cargo, specialists, medical/recruitment systems or new economy.

The compact cabin/berth and modest local response distinguish Salyut from Vortex's four-chair armed troop layout, Titan's armed cargo shuttle, Dynamic's proposed faster gunship and Utopia's proposed slower dropship. Halyard keeps its long-haul cruise/specialist/bunk capabilities; Jovian keeps freight and engineer utility. Salyut gives up weapons, freight and specialists entirely. These are proposed product tradeoffs, not historical subtype claims.
