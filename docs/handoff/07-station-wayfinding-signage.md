## Problem
Build one coherent wayfinding layer driven by the live `StationRouteRegistry`:
- **Where:** at each hub junction and module threshold the embodied player passes, a panel names the reachable destinations with arrows.
- **Destinations (registry names):** Fleet Docks and berths, Habitat, Aft Operations, VIP Reception, Activity Board, Destination Board console, Observation, Salvage Terrace.
- **Placement:** eye height, clear of walk paths and door swings, facing the approach.
- **Sign family:** one colour-safe family. Every row has a shape, text and an arrow on a high-contrast plate.
- **Geometry:** textured quads (2 triangles each), one shared material, and one atlas texture generated by a deterministic script and registered in ASSETS.md.
- **Budget:** at most 60 signs, at most +400 triangles, 1 material, no lights, no collision, no animation, batched into a few renderers.
- **Collision rule:** a sign that would sit inside another module's collision is skipped at build time and reported.

## Failure scenario (current main)
1. Start the game, spawn at CentralJunction and walk toward the Aft, Habitat or Fabrication connectors.
2. No panel says which way Fleet Docks, Habitat, Aft Operations, VIP Reception, the boards, Observation or Salvage Terrace lies.
3. At module thresholds (for example `fabrication_annex_inbound`, where Observation and Salvage branch off) nothing names what is ahead.

## What to do
Recommended approach: finish the in-flight design described below.
- **New component:** `scripts/world/station_wayfinding_signage.gd` (`class_name StationWayfindingSignage extends Node3D`).
- **Build stage:** add one stage `[&"_build_station_wayfinding", "Posting station wayfinding"]` directly after `_restore_station_activity_state` and before `_apply_sign_geometry_budget`. At that point all collision exists and moving bodies are at their start poses.
- **Placements:** derive them only from registry data, never hand-authored coordinates. There are three site types:
  - one directory at the spawn;
  - one threshold panel per registry edge, between the hub anchor and the module slot marker;
  - junction panels at chosen internal route markers.
- **Batching:** one ArrayMesh in one MeshInstance3D with a `material_override` StandardMaterial3D that samples one atlas. Unshaded, no shadow casting, GI disabled.
- **Collision check:** oriented-box test of the panel and post against every solid CollisionShape3D, with walk clearance and a larger door/moving-body clearance. Skip sites that are blocked and report them.
- **Acceptable alternatives:**
  - Several MeshInstance3D chunks (for example one per module) if visibility culling needs it. Keep the total to "few" renderers and one material.
  - A PNG atlas instead of an SVG, if the generator output is committed and deterministic.

Contracts that must not change:
- `StationRouteRegistry` and its report format: read it, do not extend it, no schema bump.
- The order of existing BUILD_STAGES entries. Insert only.
- The `SignGeometryBudget` behaviour.
- The `_consolidate_station_dressing` roster.
- Existing module collision, markers and node names.
- Do not add TextMesh or Label3D.
